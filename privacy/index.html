Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 · Vi Truong Le

Tidy Moments ("the App") is developed by Vi Truong Le ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it. We designed Tidy Moments to work entirely on your device, and this policy reflects exactly what the App does — nothing more.

The short version

Tidy Moments processes your photos and videos entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Your photos, the information about them, and anything the App learns from them never leave your device and are never sent to us or to any third party. We do not operate a server that receives your photos. We do not use advertising or analytics software in this version of the App.

Information the App accesses

Your Photo Library. To let you review and tidy your Camera Roll, the App requests access to your Photo Library. With that access, the App:

  • Reads your photos, videos, and their metadata (such as the date/time taken and, if present, the location where a photo was taken) to group them into "Moments" (an event or trip).
  • Uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to estimate photo quality and detect blur, duplicates, bursts, and screenshots, so it can suggest which photos are safe to remove.
  • Reads storage size information for photos so the space you recover is measured, not estimated.

All of this analysis happens locally, using Apple's on-device frameworks. The App does not upload your photos, video, metadata, or any information derived from them to any server operated by us or by a third party.

Deleting photos. When you accept a "tidy" action, the App asks iOS to delete the photos you approved. Those photos go into your device's built-in Recently Deleted album, exactly as if you had deleted them yourself in the Photos app, and can be recovered there for the period iOS provides (currently up to 30 days) before they are permanently erased by iOS. The App has no separate copy of deleted photos and cannot recover them once iOS has permanently erased them.

Recap Cards you choose to share. The App can generate a "Recap Card" (a still image or short video) from photos in a Moment you tidied. Recap Cards are created and stored on your device. If you tap Share, iOS's standard share sheet opens and you choose where the Recap Card goes. We do not receive a copy of anything you share, and sharing is never automatic.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the App does not contain functionality to collect:

  • Account information — the App has no accounts, sign-in, or sign-up.
  • Contact, health, financial, or browsing data.
  • Precise device location beyond what may already be embedded in a photo's existing metadata (used only on-device, as described above).
  • Advertising identifiers or data for ad targeting — the App does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK.
  • Usage analytics or crash-reporting data sent to us or to any vendor — the App does not include any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Because nothing described above is transmitted off your device, we do not share information with third parties, and there is no "data sale" of any kind.

Children's privacy

Tidy Moments is a general-audience photo-organization tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — the App does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age, off the device.

Your choices and controls

  • Revoke Photo Library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Tidy Moments.
  • Delete local App data by deleting the App from your device; this removes any Recap Cards or cached data the App stored locally.
  • Because the App does not transmit or store your data on any server we control, there is no remote account or dataset for us to export or delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's data practices change. We will update the "Effective date" above and, for material changes, provide notice within the App before the change takes effect. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
