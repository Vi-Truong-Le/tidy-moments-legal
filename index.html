Keep the memory

Every tidy-up creates a Recap card from the shots you kept — export it as an image or a short video, and keep them on your Shelf.

Private by design

Grouping, best-shot picks and deletion all run on your device. No account, no analytics, no ad tracking — nothing ever leaves your phone.

Honest numbers

The space you free is measured for real — no scary fake “storage almost full” alerts to push you into deleting.

Delete with confidence

“One tap” never means “delete everything.” Tidy Moments only removes the clutter it flags — and you stay in control.

  • Your Favorites are never auto-selected for removal
  • Videos and single, non-duplicate photos are always kept
  • Review and fine-tune every pick before you confirm
  • Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted — restorable for 30 days
