Common questions

Does Tidy Moments upload my photos anywhere?

No. All photo analysis, tidying, and Recap Card creation happen entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to any third-party server.

I deleted photos by mistake — can I get them back?

Photos removed through Tidy Moments go to your device's Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for the period iOS provides (currently up to 30 days).

Do I need an account to use the App?

No. Tidy Moments has no sign-up or login — it works directly with your device's Photo Library.

How do I manage or cancel Tidy Moments Pro?

Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple — see our Terms of Use for details.
